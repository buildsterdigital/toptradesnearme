Before you hire a plumber in Whittlesey

  • Any gas work must be done by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Check their number on the official Gas Safe Register before they start.
  • A gas boiler service costs around £80 to £120 locally.
  • Landlords need a gas safety certificate (CP12) every 12 months.
  • For a new boiler, ask which manufacturer warranty you get and whether it needs a yearly service to stay valid.

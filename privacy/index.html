Privacy notice

Last updated 2 November 2026

Top Trades Near Me is run by Buildster Digital, 12 Red Bridge Hollow, Oxford OX1 4XS. Questions about your data: listings@toptradesnearme.co.uk.

If you ask for quotes

We collect your name, phone, email (if given), postcode and the job you describe. We use them to pass your job to up to three local firms who do that kind of work, and to check the request with you. The legal basis is your request. Those firms then contact you directly and handle your details under their own privacy terms.

If you run a listed business

Business listings use publicly available details such as name, area, services and public review ratings. If you claim a listing or ask to be listed, we use the details you send to update your listing and to contact you about it, including sponsored spots. You can ask us to change or remove a listing at any time.

Who processes it

Form entries are stored in our customer system, HighLevel (LeadConnector), which acts as our processor. We do not sell your details.

How long we keep it

Quote requests are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted. Business contact details are kept while the listing is active and for 12 months after.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing listings@toptradesnearme.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Top Trades Near Me

Local trades ranked by public reviews. Run by Buildster Digital, Oxford.

How we rank: rating out of the maximum, weighted by how many reviews it is based on. Firms without a checked rating are listed after, in no order. Sponsored listings are paid placements, always labelled, and sit outside the ranking.
